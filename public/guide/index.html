User guide

Shop the way you actually walk the store.

Family Grocery keeps one shared list, sorted into the order you'd walk each store's aisles — so nothing sends you back to produce after you've already reached the checkout. This guide covers every screen, every icon, and what each badge on screen actually means.

01

Getting started

There's no sign-up. One shared password unlocks the whole household's list.

  • 1
    Password fieldTap "Show" to reveal what you typed — useful on a shared/awkward keyboard.
  • 2
    Unlock / CreateIf a password already exists for this household, this button says "Unlock." If nobody has set one yet, the whole screen instead reads "Choose a family password" and this button says "Create" — whoever does that first sets it for everyone.
💡
Only set it once. Once a password exists, it can't be re-created this way — a family member who forgets it needs whoever manages the deployment to reset it for them.

After you unlock

You land on Shop. The app now works from data stored on your device — closing the browser, losing signal, or going offline doesn't lose anything. Add it to your Home Screen for the full app-like feel (see Settings), or just keep using it as a normal browser tab — both work identically.

02

The four tabs

Everything lives behind four tabs, always reachable at the bottom of the screen.

Shop
Today's checklist — what's left to buy, grouped and ordered by aisle for whichever store you pick.
Products
Your master catalog — everything you've ever bought or might buy. Tick items on here to put them on the Shop list.
Stores
The shops you visit, and the walking order of their aisles — drag to arrange.
Settings
Password, sync, theme, catalog import/export, and app updates. It's the 4th tab, on the far right — not a separate header icon.
03

Shop

This is the screen you actually use standing in the aisle. Nothing here is for adding items — that happens on Products. Shop is purely the ordered checklist.

  • 1
    Store pickerShows "7/10" — of the 10 items on your whole list, 7 are sold at Lidl. Tap it to switch stores or jump to "Any store" (everything, grouped by store instead of aisle).
  • 2
    Progress bar + Finish"0 of 7 in cart," filling in as you check items off. Finish ends the trip.
  • 3
    Aisle groupsProduce, Bakery, Meat & Fish, Dairy & Eggs, Pantry… — in the exact walking order set on the Stores tab. The ×1 / ×2 after each name is how many items are in that group.
  • 4
    Check circleTap to mark an item in-cart — it fills, strikes through, then slides out of view.
  • 1
    The store picker, openTap the pill in the header to get here. "Any store" shows everything, grouped by store instead of aisle — a planning view, not what you'd use while actually walking one store. Each store also shows how many items on your list it covers.

Actions on an item row

Tap the circle
Marks it in-cart. It briefly fills and strikes through, then (unless the eye toggle is on) slides out of view.
Tap the circle again
Only works while it's still visible (eye toggle on) — puts it back on the "to buy" list.
Swipe left
Reveals a red Remove action — removes it from today's list entirely (not from the catalog). Undo toast follows.
Long-press (~0.5s)
Opens the item sheet: edit this trip's quantity/note, move it to a different aisle at this store, or remove it from the list.
Tap the row itself
Same as long-press — opens the item sheet.
  • 1
    Qty / NoteOverrides the catalog default for this trip only — e.g. "12" instead of the usual "6" this one time.
  • 2
    Aisle at this storeMoves the item to a different aisle at the currently selected store, without opening the full Products sheet.
  • 3
    Remove from listSame as swiping the row — takes it off today's list, not the catalog.
💡
Pull down to refresh. At the top of the list, pull down to force a sync with other devices right away, instead of waiting for it to happen automatically.
04

Products

Your master catalog. This is where you add new items, and where you decide which stores/aisles sell each one. This screen looks and behaves differently depending on whether a store is selected.

  • 1
    Store pickerOn "All" (no store), you see the flat catalog, name-sorted. Pick a store and the screen switches to that store's items grouped by aisle — the same order they'll appear on Shop.
  • 2
    SearchFilters by name or note as you type. Selecting a store while searching temporarily shows the flat list, so search always looks across everything.
  • 3
    Circle iconTap the green to add a product to today's list; tap the red to take it back off. Either way, this never touches the catalog entry itself, only whether it's currently "needed."
  • 4
    + button (bottom right)Add a brand-new product to the catalog.

With a store selected: arranging aisles

Products placed at that store appear grouped under their aisle name, in the order you'll walk them. Drag any row by its grip (the dots on the right) to reorder it within its aisle, or move it to a different aisle via the item's own sheet. This view is the one place that actually sets the walking order used on Shop.

  • 1
    Aisle headingsProduce, Bakery, Meat & Fish… — plain labels here (no item count), matching the order set on Stores.
  • 2
    Drag handleThe six dots on the right of each row — press and drag to reorder within the aisle.

Tap a row → Product sheet

  • 1
    Name / Qty / NoteThe catalog defaults — e.g. always "semi-skimmed." They autosave as you type.
  • 2
    Buy atOne checkbox per store. Tick it to place this product there; the dropdown beside it then assigns a specific aisle (or leave it Unsorted).
  • 3
    Delete productRemoves it from the whole catalog — every store's placement and today's list, if it was on it. Undo toast follows.
💡
Same product, different aisle per store. "Milk" is in Dairy & Eggs at Lidl and Coop, but just Dairy at Hieber — each store's placement is independent, which is exactly what makes the Shop screen's per-store ordering work.
05

Stores

Add the shops you visit, then teach the app how you walk each one.

  • 1
    Tap a storeOpens its Store screen — the pencil icon is just a visual hint that it's editable, tapping anywhere on the row works.
  • 2
    + buttonAdd a new store by name — you'll land straight in its Store screen to start adding aisles.

Inside a store: arranging the walk

  • 1
    Drag an aisleReorders it. This order is the walking path Shop follows for this store — put them in the sequence you actually walk.
  • 2
    + Add aisleCreate a new zone, e.g. "Frozen" or "Checkout."
  • 3
    Rename (top right)Renames the store itself. Below the visible area: a Delete store button — removes its aisles and placements, but products stay in the catalog. Undo toast follows.

Tap an aisle's × to delete it — its products fall back to "Unsorted" at this store rather than disappearing.

    AppearanceSystem / Light / Dark, as a three-way switch.
  • 2
    SyncA colored dot + status text (here: "Synced just now"), and a manual Sync now button. See the badge meanings below.
  • 3
    HelpUser guide opens this very page.
  • 4
    UpdatesSee below.
  • 1
    CatalogImport / Export — see "Catalog: import & export" below.
  • 2
    SessionsSign-in log shows recent unlock attempts; Log out all devices revokes every session at once — every device, this one included, needs the password again after.

Not pictured: an Install section appears above Catalog, but only when your browser is ready to offer it — see the FAQ below.

Sync status badges

Idle / synced Syncing… Offline Sync error

The same colored dot also appears as a small pill in the header on Shop/Products/Stores whenever there's something worth flagging — synced and idle stays quiet, but "Syncing…," "Offline," or a pending-changes count shows up next to the gear icon.

Checking for updates

Check for updates asks the server which version is live without reloading anything first, so you know before you commit to it:

Already current
A quick "You're on the latest version" message — nothing else happens.
New version available
The button itself turns into New version available — tap to install. Tapping it clears this device's cache and reloads, then confirms with "Updated ✓" once the new version is actually running.

The version string underneath (e.g. 4b3e315 · 2026-09-18 18:51) is what's currently running on this device — the code revision and when it was built.

Catalog: import & export

Settings → Catalog has two buttons:

Export
Downloads the whole catalog — every store, aisle, and product — as one JSON file. Good for a backup, or as a starting point to hand to an AI assistant.
Import
Paste JSON back in, or a plain list of product names — one per line, optionally name, qty, note. Existing stores and products are matched by name, so importing twice doesn't create duplicates.
Replace
A checkbox in the Import sheet. Off merges the import into what's already there; on clears the current catalog first, so you start clean from exactly what you paste.

Building a catalog with AI

You don't have to type every product by hand — ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI assistant can write the import JSON for you. Give it a prompt like this:

Write JSON matching exactly this shape, with no other text in your reply:

{
  "stores": [{ "name": "string", "aisles": ["string", "..."] }],
  "products": [
    {
      "name": "string",
      "qty": "string, optional",
      "note": "string, optional",
      "needed": true,
      "stores": ["Store name", "Store name: Aisle name"]
    }
  ]
}

My stores and aisles: Lidl (Produce, Bakery, Dairy, Frozen), Costco (Dry
goods, Fridge wall). Generate 25 common household grocery items, placed in
the store and aisle where each is normally found. Mark milk, eggs, and bread
as needed.
💡
Export first, edit, re-import. Exporting gives you real JSON in your own catalog's exact shape — handing that to the AI instead of a blank prompt makes it easy to ask for edits like "add 10 more snacks" or "add a Coop store with these aisles" while keeping everything else intact.

Paste the reply into Settings → Catalog → Import — with or without Replace, as above. Imports are capped at 2,000 items at a time.

08

Frequently asked

Why does "Add to Home Screen" only show up sometimes?

Your browser has to confirm the app is installable before it offers the option, and that check sometimes only completes on a second load — reloading the page (or just revisiting it later) is usually all it takes for the option to appear in Settings.

I marked something as bought — where did it go?

It's hidden by design, so the list only shows what's left. Tap the eye icon in the status bar to bring bought items back into view, struck through, at the bottom of their aisle.

Can the same product be needed differently for different trips?

Yes — the item sheet on Shop lets you override the quantity or note just for this trip, without changing the catalog's default.

What happens if I "Finish shopping" by accident?

Nothing permanent right away — an 8-second Undo toast appears immediately after, restoring the exact list you had before.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes, fully. Every screen reads and writes to the device first; changes queue up and sync automatically the moment a connection comes back.

Multiple people editing at once — what wins?

The most recent change to a given item wins. Two people reordering the very same aisle at the same time while both offline can occasionally interleave oddly — reopening the app straightens it out automatically.
